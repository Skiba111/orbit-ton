How it works

Three steps.
Then billing runs itself.

No pull mechanism. No repeated approvals. No trusted hot wallet. Just a contract that holds funds and releases them on schedule.

01

Service deploys a Factory

One transaction to the Registry. A Factory contract is deployed with ORBIT fee settings enforced at the bytecode level. Add your subscription plans — price, period, trial duration.

→
02

Subscriber funds their contract

User sends OP_SUBSCRIBE to the Factory. A personal Subscription contract deploys and holds their deposit. No open-ended authorisations — they control their own contract.

→
03

Relayer charges on schedule

Every billing period, the relayer or a permissionless keeper triggers the charge. Fees split automatically: protocol fee to ORBIT, service fee to you, net to your wallet. Your webhook fires.

Features

Everything billing needs.
Nothing it doesn't.

TON & Jetton billing

Native TON or any TEP-74 token — USDT, USDC, and more. Protocol fee is always collected in TON regardless of payment type.

Deposit model

Subscribers pre-fund a personal contract. No pull payments, no custodial wallets. The service can never extract more than the agreed amount.

Keeper network

Any address can trigger charges in keeper mode and earn 0.01 TON per charge. No infrastructure required on your end — the market provides the trigger layer.

Grace period + retry

When deposit runs low, the subscription enters a 3-day grace window before cancelling — up to 5 charge retries with exponential backoff. Significantly reduces churn from transient failures.

Plan upgrade / downgrade

Subscribers can request a plan change at any time. The Factory routes it securely — you cannot be switched to a cheaper plan you didn't authorise.

Trial periods

Per-plan free trials, one-time per subscriber per Factory. Trial duration and billing period are set independently. No trial abuse — it's enforced at the contract level.

Bounce recovery

If a payment message bounces — e.g. frozen service contract — the deposit is fully restored and the billing clock rolled back. Subscribers are never silently drained.

Registry integration

One transaction — 0.3 TON — to the ORBIT Registry gets you a fully configured Factory with fee settings enforced at the bytecode level. No manual deploy required.

Architecture

Three contracts.
Fully isolated.

Each layer has a single responsibility. Subscribers interact only with their own Subscription contract — it holds their deposit and enforces the billing rules independently of everything else.

Contracts are immutable after deployment. There is no upgrade mechanism — this is a security property. Subscribers can always verify the exact bytecode their contract runs against the published hashes.

Fee model

Two fees. Fixed rules.
No surprises.

Both fees are deducted from the gross plan amount before the service receives its net. The protocol fee is hardcoded in bytecode — enforced on every charge in every deployed contract. Any contract not matching the official ORBIT code hash is immediately identifiable on-chain.

1.5%
Protocol fee
Hardcoded as PROTOCOL_FEE_BPS = 150 in Subscription bytecode. Immutable in every deployed contract — no runtime call, no configuration, no operator action can alter it. Collected in TON even for Jetton subscriptions.
0–10%
Service fee
Set by the Factory deployer at registration time via fee_bps (0–1000). Immutable after Factory deploy — no bait-and-switch possible. Enforced at the billing engine level on every charge cycle.
Example — 10 TON / month plan · service fee 3%
Gross (plan price) 10.000 TON

Security

Designed to be
adversarial-safe.

Every known attack vector is documented and tested. 83 passing tests, 5 test suites, each covering a specific threat category.

Double-charge guard
charging_in_progress flag blocks any new charge while a Jetton transfer is in flight. Only cleared on authenticated completion.
Replay protection
Every state-changing operation increments seqno. External messages include a 60-second timestamp window. Stale or replayed messages are rejected.
Storage reserve
raw_reserve(0.05 TON) called before every outgoing message. The contract always keeps enough TON for its own storage rent.
Fee immutability
fee_bps is locked at Factory deploy time. OP_UPDATE_FEE_BPS was deliberately removed. Fee routing address is immutable per Subscription.
FeeCollector timelock
24-hour two-phase withdrawal. If the owner key is compromised, there's a window to rotate the key and cancel before any funds move.
